Indication Coding in Veeva — Known Inconsistencies & Search Rules

The indication field in fact_site and fact_study is a free-text field entered by Ora study teams at the time of study setup. There is no enforced controlled vocabulary. As a result, the same clinical indication can appear under multiple label variants across studies. Any indication query that searches on a single exact term will silently miss records coded under alternative labels.

Rule: Always search using a multi-term pattern covering all known label variants, never a single exact string alone.

Known indication label variants (search all of these together):

nAMD / Wet AMD:
Wet AMD · Dry AMD · Neovascular (Wet) Age-Related Macular Degeneration · Age-Related Macular Degeneration with Geographic Atrophy · Dry AMD

Geographic Atrophy:
Geographic Atrophy · Age-Related Macular Degeneration with Geographic Atrophy

Glaucoma:
Glaucoma · Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma or Ocular Hypertension · Devices - Glaucoma · Optic neuropathies POAG and NAION

Diabetic Retinopathy:
Diabetic Retinopathy · Proliferative Diabetic Retinopathy

DME:
Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)

Dry Eye:
Dry Eye · Devices-Dry Eye · Meibomian Gland Dysfunction

Stargardt's:
Stargardt's Disease — consistent single label (safe to search alone; still include Stargardt / Stargardt Disease aliases)

LCA:
Leber congenital amaurosis — consistent

RP:
Retinitis Pigmentosa — consistent

RVO:
Macular Edema due to Retinal Vein Occlusion (RVO) · Retinal Vascular Diseases

Devices:
Devices-Diagnostic · Devices - Other · Devices - Glaucoma · Devices-Dry Eye

Standard search pattern (conceptually):
AMD = Wet AMD | Dry AMD | Neovascular.*Macular | Geographic Atrophy | Age-Related Macular
GLAUC = Glaucoma | POAG | Ocular Hypertension | OHT | Optic Neuropath
DE = Dry Eye | Meibomian | Devices-Dry Eye
RETINA = nAMD | AMD | Macular | Retina | Diabetic | DME | Stargardt | IRD | Retinitis | Choroid | RVO | Vein Occlusion | Geographic Atrophy | Inherited

INCORRECT: filtering sites where indication == 'Wet AMD' alone — misses other AMD labels.

When a new indication is queried for the first time: (1) inspect distinct indication values in Veeva, (2) identify all relevant label variants, (3) build a multi-term pattern covering all variants, (4) proceed with the filtered query.

Null PSM does not mean no Veeva data — studies/sites may still exist with study_number, sponsor, enrolled, and org_clean. Always list them.

Data quality recommendation: Veeva data hygiene should standardise indication coding to a controlled vocabulary. Priority: AMD variants, glaucoma variants, and device study labelling. Stargardt's, LCA, and RP are already consistent.
